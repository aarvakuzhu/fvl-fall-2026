Every team plays 5 guaranteed games — no early exits, no dead brackets.

Format

  • 01Round 1: Pool Play (3 games) — random draw into 4 pools of 4, round robin.
  • 02Tier Assignment — pool results sort every team into a tier: Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Copper.
  • 03Round 2: Tiered Bracket (2 games) — each tier runs its own semifinal, then a final, deciding tier champion.
  • 04Order of play — Copper and Bronze semis go first, Silver and Gold follow, with the Gold Final closing out the day.
  • 058 prizes on the line — a champion (and runner-up, where applicable) crowned in every tier, plus the Iron Will Trophy for the tournament's toughest finish.
